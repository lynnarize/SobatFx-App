## ✓ fib
**Prompt:** Which Fibonacci retracement levels do traders watch most?

The most-watched Fibonacci retracement levels are **0.382**, **0.500**, **0.618** and **0.786**.  
- 0.382 – shallow pullback, trend still strong.  
- 0.500 – not a Fib number but universally used; halfway retracement.  
- 0.618 – “golden ratio”, deep pullback that often holds if the trend is intact.  
- 0.786 – final defence; a close beyond it often signals trend change.

Traders draw the tool from the **swing low to swing high** in an uptrend (or swing high to swing low in a downtrend) and watch price react at those levels, especially when they line up with structure, EMAs or order blocks.
